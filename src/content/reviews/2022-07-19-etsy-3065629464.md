I got 3 of these in different lengths and colors and i am so happy i did. They are all strong enough to hold my hair in place all day, they’re sleek and unobtrusive, are matte and not slippery (some shiny finish hair forks slide right out of my hair), the gentle curve is just right, and they are much lighter weight than my other metal hair forks. 

I also appreciate that they are titanium because I have many metal allergies but I can tolerate titanium easily. 

I would be thrilled if these came in a wavy version, making them even less likely to slip out.
