It can be hard to find hair accessories that are elegant enough for a professional environment yet sturdy and gentle on the hair - let alone waterproof. Kathrin’s pieces easily check all these boxes and are some of the most beautiful options on the market today. 

Kathrin’s attention to detail extends to the fabulous packaging, which reinforces that her pieces are truly hair jewelry and not your usual accessories. Shipping to the US was faster than expected and tracked via USPS. 

The hair comma is light as a feather and the wide, flat shape is particularly well suited to center-held buns. The shape requires a bit of practice to manage (and my hair is a bit short at the moment), but when I get the tension right, the hair comma makes even my amateurish buns look put together.
