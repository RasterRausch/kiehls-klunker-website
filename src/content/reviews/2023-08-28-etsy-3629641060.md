Most products are “good enough” versions of what we actually want, under more ideal circumstances. But every once in a while, you find something that’s just perfect and you no longer need to ever look for anything else in that category again. That is this hairstick for me.

It’s perfect. It holds the bun so it doesn’t have to jut out on either side of my head like a spike and get caught on things, it’s made of a low-maintenance material I don’t have to worry about even working around salt water, and it doesn’t move all day.

I bought one just to see if it was as awesome as it looked. It is, and now I bought one for every room in my house so I’m never without one.

This is the Platonic form of hair sticks. Also Kathrin was very helpful when I messed up my second order. Thanks for your help.
